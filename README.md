UI/UX Portfolio

A collection of UI/UX projects and prototypes that I have worked on in Figma.

These projects were created as part of different hackathons, so they are currently prototypes focused on exploring the idea, user flow, interface, and overall experience rather than fully developed applications.

CropSaathi

CropSaathi is a platform designed to help farmers with everyday farming decisions and make useful agricultural information easier to access.

Some of the ideas explored in the prototype include:

- Plant recommendations based on factors such as location, season, and soil conditions
- Step-by-step instructions for farming and crop care
- Information about ideal conditions needed for different plants
- Latest agricultural news and updates
- Information about government schemes and agricultural resources
- An AI chatbot to help farmers with their questions

Designed in: Figma

Healique

Healique is a mental wellness app concept focused on helping users express their emotions, relax, track their moods, and feel less alone.

Mood Cloak
A way to express how you feel without having to put everything into words. Users can create a daily mood board using things like drawings, paintings, pictures, videos, music, and emojis.

Mind & Body Relaxation
Includes simple relaxation techniques such as guided breathing exercises with calming visuals and the 5-4-3-2-1 grounding technique.

Anonymous Chat
An anonymous space where users can talk to others, share what they are feeling, and feel less alone.

Mood Tracking
- Daily mood tracking
- Mood charts
- A personal dashboard to look back at mood patterns

Designed in: Figma

[View Healique Prototype](https://www.figma.com/design/t6rzResCTfbBV4wJINLRCm/Healique?node-id=0-1&t=ucL47R38QdCNdw0V-1)


PolicyGuru

PolicyGuru is a platform designed to make government schemes and policies easier to find and understand.

The idea is to help users find schemes that may be relevant to them based on information such as their age, gender, state, income range, and occupation.

Some of the features explored in the prototype include:

- Personalized government scheme recommendations
- Regional and multilingual support
- Government policies explained in simple, everyday language instead of complicated terminology
- Search option to find specific schemes and policies
- Help desk for assistance
- User feedback options

Designed in:Figma
